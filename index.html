The Gateway to the Sahara

An Ancient Oasis at the Edge of Silence

Between the Anti-Atlas mountains and the boundless dunes lies Foum Zguid — a palm-cradled oasis on the old caravan routes, and the most elegant door to the Moroccan Sahara.

From here we lead you across the white mirror of Lake Iriki, over fossil-strewn plains and into the golden cathedral of Erg Chegaga. Days move at camel pace; nights belong to the fire, the drums and a sky with no end.

Foum Zguid Trip is a family house of desert guides. Every journey is private, every detail considered — from the weave of your tent to the last glass of mint tea.

“The desert does not hurry, yet everything is accomplished.”— Saharan proverb
